This page takes you from the download to your first note in a few minutes. You don't need an account to write; sign in only for Zditor AI and membership features.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Zditor">
    Download the version for your system from the [download page](https://zditor.com/download).

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="macOS">
        Choose **Apple silicon** for Macs with an M-series chip and **Intel** for older Macs. Open the `.dmg` disk image, drag Zditor into your Applications folder, and open it from there.
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Windows">
        Run the `.exe` setup program. The `.msi` package is meant for managed installs.
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Linux">
        Make the AppImage executable with `chmod +x` and run it, or install the `.deb` or `.rpm` package with your package manager.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go through the setup">
    The first time Zditor opens, a short setup walks you through four steps:

    - **Welcome**: click **Set up Zditor**.
    - **Interface**: choose a comfortable **Interface size**.
    - **Workspace**: choose where your work lives, as described in the next step.
    - **Agent**: connect the AI service the built-in agent uses, or click **Skip and finish** to do it later.

    You can change all of this later. To see the setup again, click **Restart tour** in **Settings → Personal → General**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a workspace">
    A workspace is the folder Zditor works in. Its files appear in the sidebar, and Zditor keeps them in their original format.

    - **Local folder**: click **Choose folder** and pick a folder on your computer.
    - **S3**, **WebDAV**, or **SSH**: enter the connection details to work on files stored elsewhere.

    Later, the workspace menu at the top of the **Files** panel switches between workspaces, and an empty Agent tab offers **Open Workspace** and **New Workspace**. Remote connections, including Git repositories, are managed in **Settings → Integrations → Connections**. See [the file tree guide](/en/file-tree) for more.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write your first note">
    Click **New File** at the top of the file tree, type a name, and press `Enter`. New files end in `.md` unless you change the default.

    Then start typing. Markdown turns into formatting as you type: `## ` at the start of a line makes a heading, for example. Type `/` at the start of a line for the command menu, `@` to link another file, and `:` for emoji. Zditor saves your changes to the file automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect the agent (optional)">
    The built-in agent needs an AI service. Open **Settings → AI → Agents** and set **AI service** to one of:

    - **Zditor**: sign in to your Zditor account. This needs Pro or free AI tokens on your account.
    - **Custom provider**: choose the **API protocol** (OpenAI Responses or Claude Messages), enter the **Base URL** and **API key**, click **Fetch models**, enable a model, and click **Save**.

    Then click **+** in the tab bar to open an Agent tab and send a message. See the [Agent Guide](/en/agent-guide) for the details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
